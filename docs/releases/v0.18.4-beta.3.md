## Conduit v0.18.4-beta.3

This is a beta. It fixes the problems found while testing beta.2. Try double-clicking entries, signing out of a remote desktop, and using a narrow side bar.

### Fixed: tabs, double-click and gateways
- **Title bar**: with the Beta pill showing, the side bar's top buttons no longer spill over the Home tab. On a Mac the pill now sits next to the vault name.
- **RD Gateways look like gateways**: the info view, its tab, the sidebar, search results and Home now say "RD Gateway" with the gateway icon, not "Credential" with a key. Home counts gateways apart from credentials.
- **Double-click a credential or gateway** to open its editor. Before, nothing happened.
- **Double-click an entry that is already open** to switch to its tab. Before, SSH opened a second tab and RDP reconnected and dropped your session. To open another SSH or web session on purpose, right-click the entry and pick Open New Session.
- **Ending a session closes its tab**: typing "exit" in SSH, or signing out of a remote desktop, now closes the tab instead of showing "Connection Error". A dropped connection still shows the error, and a dropped remote desktop reconnects when you open it again.
