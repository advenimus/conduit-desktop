## Conduit v0.19.0

Conduit's account and sync service moved to new servers that we run ourselves. Your vaults, backups, teams and plan came along, and the app works the way it did before.

### Good to know
- You need to sign in once after updating. Use the same email and password as before.
- If you use an authenticator app, the first sign-in sends a code to your email instead. Then you scan a new QR code, and your authenticator works as before.
- Older versions of Conduit show "Update required" and stop syncing until you update.

### Under the hood
- The backend is now a self-hosted Appwrite server in place of Supabase, with the same rules for plans, devices, teams and backups.
