## Conduit v0.18.0

This release lets you keep one vault up to date on all your devices. It adds a Home dashboard, a cleaner look with a choice of icon packs, and up to three AI agents at once. Free plans now get unlimited MCP tool calls.

### Highlights
- **Your vault on every device**: keep a personal vault in iCloud Drive, OneDrive, Dropbox, Google Drive or a network share, and Conduit merges changes field by field.
- **More on Free**: MCP tool calls are now unlimited. A Free vault is open on one device at a time, and "Use here instead" moves it to the computer you are using. Pro and Team can keep a vault open on up to 5 devices at once.
- **Home dashboard**: a new first tab with a quick search bar, recent and open connections, favorites, AI activity, vault status and anything that needs attention. Press Cmd+Shift+H (Ctrl+Shift+H on Windows and Linux) to get back to it.
- **A fresh look**: a compact new style, a neutral "Modern" color scheme, and six icon packs in Settings > Appearance: Lucide (the new default), Phosphor, Hugeicons, Material Symbols, Fluent and the classic Tabler. Custom entry icons that have a match follow the pack you pick.
- **Three agents at once**: run up to three AI agent panes side by side. Each pane picks its own engine, and agents stay out of each other's sessions.

### New
- **Conflict review**: when two devices change the same field, you see both values and pick one. The sidebar shows "N to review" and conflicted items get an amber dot.
- **Recently deleted**: deleted items on a synced vault can be restored, or erased for good on every device.
- **Settings > Sync**: shows your plan, a Sync now button, your devices and the review tools.
- **Safety nets for sync**: big changes get an automatic snapshot with Undo, and Conduit shows a preview before it merges a copy that could revert or delete data.
- **Other copies**: "conflicted copy" files from your cloud drive are found and merged only when that is safe. Nothing is moved or deleted without your click.
- **One owner per vault**: use "Make my own copy" to fork a vault you do not own, or "Release this vault" in Settings > Sync to hand it over.
- **Open at startup**: pick a vault in Settings > General and Conduit opens it instead of the Vault Hub.
- **Unlock automatically at startup**: let this computer unlock your startup vault using the system keychain, after a clear warning. Hold Shift (Option on a Mac) while Conduit starts to skip it once.
- **Lock when idle**: choose 5, 15, 30 or 60 minutes in Settings > Security. The vault also locks when your screen locks. It is off by default.
- **Folder view**: right-click a folder and choose View Info for a searchable list of everything in it, with Open all and Check all.
- **Is it up?**: a quick check of whether a host answers, in folder view and in entry info. Entry info also lists your last 20 connections to that entry.
- **Pin the sidebar**: dock it beside your sessions or let it float and hide itself. Cmd/Ctrl+B shows or hides it, and Cmd/Ctrl+Shift+B switches between pinned and floating.
- **Agent terminal keys**: Shift+Enter adds a new line, and Cmd/Ctrl plus or minus zooms only the agent pane you are in.

### Improved
- **Cleaner window on macOS**: the title bar joins the top row, tabs become rounded pills, and every tab keeps its close button and status dot.
- **One compact style**: buttons, fields, lists, menus, dialogs and toasts now share the same look, with stronger text contrast and a visible focus ring for keyboard use.
- **Quick Unlock follows your vault**: Touch ID still works after you rename or move a synced vault, and a saved password made stale by a change on another device is cleared and re-asked.
- **Backup restore is safer on synced vaults**: restoring into a synced vault shows a preview with a rollback option instead of a file overwrite that the next merge would undo.
- **Master password changes reach every device**: other devices ask for the new password, and you can also erase Recently deleted items when you change it.
- **Moved shared files**: if a synced vault file is renamed or moved in your cloud folder, Conduit finds it again or offers to locate it.
- **Agent setup per tool**: each agent folder now gets the right Conduit MCP config for Claude Code, Codex, Grok, Cursor, Gemini and OpenCode.
- **Better error messages**: about 30 places now say why something failed, such as a wrong current password, instead of a general message.
- **Liquid Glass icon on macOS**: the app icon now has light, dark, clear and tinted looks.
- **Team invites**: accepting an invite now checks seats on the server, and a full team or ended plan tells you why.

### Fixed
- **Remote clipboard**: copy and paste with RDP sessions no longer stalls on Linux, and files copied from a remote desktop paste on macOS without missing items.
- **AI terminal tools**: multi-line scripts, heredocs and long output now work, output without a final newline no longer hangs, and agents get the exit code with clean text.
- **Full-screen programs**: agents can read screens like vim and top, and send keys and wait for the reply.
- **Local Network warning**: the macOS "blocking local network access" message now shows only when a host is truly unreachable, not for sign-in failures, refusals or timeouts.
- **Toasts in the credential picker** now show, and dialogs now draw above live web views.
- **Long vault names** no longer run under the sidebar buttons.
- **RDM import**: overwriting an entry now keeps the old username or password in its history.

### Good to know
- **Free plan**: a personal vault can be open on one device at a time. Opening it elsewhere asks "Use here instead", and the other device saves and locks (its open sessions keep running).
- **Device limit**: each account can have personal vaults open on up to 5 devices at once. A sixth device locks the one you used least recently.
- **Vault owners**: a personal vault belongs to the first account that opens it online. Another account can use it for 14 days, then needs its own copy.
- **Cloud backup**: it is Pro and Team only. Pro keeps up to 25 snapshots from the last 14 days for up to 10 vaults, clearing the oldest to make room. Team keeps 6 months with no limit. Local backup stays on every plan.
- **Platform themes are gone**: the macOS, Windows and Ubuntu themes are replaced by the new look. Your settings move over once and your old icon style carries over as an icon pack.
- **Sync is always on**: there is no on/off switch in Settings. Older desktops (0.17 and earlier) can still open the file, and their edits are merged in.
- **Older desktops**: if one keeps side files next to a synced vault, publishing pauses until you confirm Conduit is closed on your other computers.
- **Phone and tablet**: use the iPhone and iPad app 1.1 or newer for sync on mobile.
- **Update notices**: the server can require a newer app. You will see "Update required" until you update.
- **Auto unlock is opt in**: nothing is saved unless you turn it on, and it is refused on Linux without a real keyring. Signing out or removing the vault from recents turns it off.

### Under the hood
- **Electron 44**: Conduit moves off Electron 41, which reached end of life, and Mac packages now carry only their own platform's native files.
- **Hardening**: packaged builds turn off the debugger and NODE_OPTIONS so other programs cannot run Conduit's signed app to reach saved secrets, and sign-in tokens are kept out of logs.
