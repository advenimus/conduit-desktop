## Conduit v0.18.1

This release adds a knowledge base for every asset, so you and your AI agents can keep what you know about each system in one place. It also keeps passwords in your notes encrypted and out of your agents' sight.

### New
- Every asset has a Knowledge tab for overviews, facts, procedures, troubleshooting, contacts and change logs, each with its own history you can compare and restore.
- Folder articles show on every asset inside that folder, and vault playbooks show on every asset that shares one of their tags.
- A Knowledge button at the bottom of the side bar opens a page to search and filter all articles, with a list of items waiting for review.
- Agents read the knowledge before they start and write down what they learn. Their edits get a blue dot, and you can review the change and keep it or undo it.
- Articles open as sub-tabs inside the tab you opened them from, like browser tabs.
- Type !!password!! in notes, documents or articles (or use the key button) and Conduit stores it encrypted and shows a chip. You can reveal it, copy it, or type it into a session.
- Settings > Security has an "Encrypt secrets in all notes" button, and asset pages warn about unencrypted or unused secrets.
- When an asset's notes look like a knowledge base, a banner lets you split them into articles by heading or ask your agent to organize them.
- The Home page has an Agent learnings card showing recent agent edits and how many are waiting for review.
- Agents can now change part of an entry's notes without rewriting the whole thing.

### Improved
- Agents no longer see your passwords. They use references that Conduit swaps for the real value, and terminal and page output is scrubbed.
- When an agent asks to see a password, a dialog asks you to Allow once or Deny. No answer within 60 seconds counts as Deny.
- Folder pages show entries beside the folder's knowledge, and the Knowledge page shows the review list beside all articles, when the window is wide enough.
- Exports keep entries nested under other entries, and imports restore that nesting.
- Duplicating an entry copies the secrets its notes use. Deleting an entry deletes them.

### Fixed
- Command entries now import from .conduit-export files.
- Agent password approval was not working before. The approval dialog now appears.
- Adding a secret from the pop-up no longer saves and closes the entry dialog.
- The unused-secret warning no longer lingers after notes link the secret.
- The article kind picker no longer snaps back to the old kind.
- Editing one article on two devices now leaves only the article text to review. Conduit settles the rest on its own.
- Agents no longer save a password as the word "[REDACTED]" when they rewrite notes.
- Knowledge articles no longer show up as saved connections in the agent's connection list.
- Agents now save facts you give them about an asset in its knowledge base, not in their own memory files.

### Under the hood
- New users are added to the Resend contact list at signup, and feedback emails now send through Resend. Plus new live tests and a shared data contract for the knowledge base.
