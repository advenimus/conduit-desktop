## Conduit v0.18.3

This release lets you try new features early with beta updates.

### New
- Settings > General has a new "Get beta updates" option. Turn it on to try new features before they reach everyone.
- Beta updates use your normal account and data. Nothing needs to move.
- Turn it off anytime. You stay on the beta you have and move to the next regular release when it comes out.
- Beta builds show a small Beta label in the title bar and the About dialog. Click it to send feedback.

### Fixed
- Small bug fixes and improvements.
